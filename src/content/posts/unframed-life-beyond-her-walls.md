​This series captures the quiet metamorphosis of life after exile. It tracks the shift from hypervigilance to genuine ease, detailing how the former scapegoat dismantles the false narrative thrust upon them, reclaims their personal agency, and builds a grounded, peaceful existence outside the abusive circle.
